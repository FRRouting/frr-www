---
title: Security / Responsible Disclosure
---

Vulnerabilities discovered by the FRR team affecting older versions of FRR are
patched and documented using CVEs.  NetDEF functions as CNA (CVE Numbering
Authority) for the FRRouting project and maintains a [list of CVE IDs
related to FRR.](https://cna.netdef.org/frrouting/cve/).
